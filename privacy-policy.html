Privacy Policy for ElfAnt — Never Forget

Effective Date: May 28, 2026
Developer: DigiPeace LLC

Overview

ElfAnt ("the App") is a personal productivity application developed by DigiPeace LLC. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

ElfAnt stores the following data exclusively on your device using local storage (Hive):

  • Tasks, habits, and goals you create
  • Your profile information (name, experience points, streaks, preferences)
  • Notification settings and sound preferences
  • App theme preferences

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the backup/export feature.

Google Account Data (Optional)

If you choose to connect your Google account to sync with Google Calendar, ElfAnt accesses:

  • Your Google account email address (to identify your account)
  • Your Google Calendar events (to import and sync tasks)

This data is used solely to provide the Calendar sync feature. ElfAnt does not store your Google credentials. Authentication is handled securely through Google Sign-In.

You can disconnect your Google account at any time in Settings → Google Calendar.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not collect crash reports or diagnostic data
  • We do not use advertising networks
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties

Third-Party Services

ElfAnt integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In / Google Calendar API — used only when you enable Calendar sync. Google Privacy Policy
  • In-App Review — used to request App Store / Play Store reviews at appropriate moments. No personal data is shared. Google Privacy Policy | Apple Privacy Policy

Data Backup and Export

ElfAnt allows you to export a backup of your data as a JSON file. This file contains your tasks, habits, goals, and preferences. You are solely responsible for the security of exported backup files. Exported backups do not include payment or Pro status information.

Notifications

ElfAnt sends local notifications for task reminders, habit check-ins, and Pomodoro sessions. These notifications are processed entirely on your device. No notification data is transmitted to external servers.

You can disable notifications at any time in your device Settings → Notifications → ElfAnt.

Children's Privacy

ElfAnt is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
