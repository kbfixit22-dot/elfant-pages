Description of Service

ElfAnt is a personal productivity application developed by DigiPeace LLC. It helps you:

  • Create, schedule, and complete tasks and recurring habits
  • Write journal and gratitude entries
  • Run focus/Pomodoro timers, including ones started hands-free via Siri
  • Optionally sync tasks and events with Google Calendar
  • Optionally receive tasks from trusted contacts via a Telegram bot

Your task, journal, and profile data is stored locally on your device, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Google Calendar Access

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, ElfAnt requests permission to read and write calendar events on your behalf, solely to show your events in the App and let you push tasks to your calendar. You can disconnect this access at any time in Settings → Google Calendar, or from your Google Account's third-party access controls.

Telegram Integration

If you enable it, trusted contacts you approve may send you tasks by messaging ElfAnt's Telegram bot. You control who is trusted, and you can revoke a contact's access at any time.
