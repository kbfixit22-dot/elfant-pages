Using ElfAnt

A tour of the app — how it's laid out and what each part does.

ElfAnt (Never Forget) is a gamified task, habit, and goal tracker with Memo — a friendly elephant who nudges, celebrates, and keeps you honest. Offline-first, with optional Google Calendar sync and a way to share tasks with anyone, no account required.

Offline-first Google Calendar sync Siri quick-capture No account to share a task

02 · The Main Screen — Your Home Screen

Everything starts here: today's tasks, your habits, pinned goals, and quick access to every other part of the app along the top.

Search
Find any task instantly, done or not.
Scan
Import a task someone shared with you as a card.
Weekly Review
See how your week went at a glance.
Focus Timer
Start a Pomodoro session on any task, habit, or goal.
Goals
Must/Should/Could/Won't-Do goals, pin up to 5 here.
Journal
Daily reflections with text, photos, or GIFs.
Sync Calendar
Pull in today's events from Google Calendar.
Profile
XP, level, streak, and settings.

How the lists work

Overdue, Today, and Upcoming each rank by priority first, then by scheduled time, and show only your top 5 — a "+N more" link expands the rest. Check a Today task off and it disappears immediately, with the next-ranked task sliding into its place and a small green badge tracking how many you've finished today.

03 · What You Can Do — Core Features

Everything below is reachable from the Home screen's top bar or the task editor.

Tasks & Priorities
Set a due time and a Low/Medium/High priority. Drag to reorder your visible top 5, or let the ranking do it for you.
Habits
Daily habit sections with streaks — check in once a day; miss one and the streak resets.
Goals
Sort what matters into Must / Should / Could / Won't Do, and pin up to 5 to your Home strip.
Focus Timer
Pomodoro sessions from 5 to 60 minutes. Completed sessions log focus time straight onto the linked task.
Journal
A private daily entry — text, photos, or GIFs, stored right on your device.
Calendar Sync
Two-way Google Calendar sync — today's events show up as tasks, and new tasks push back to your calendar.
Share & Scan
Turn a task into a postable card with an embedded QR. Text it, post it — the recipient scans it straight into their own ElfAnt.
Gamification
Earn XP and coins for every completed task, level up, and keep your streak alive. Memo reacts to how your day's going.
Siri Quick-Capture
"Add a task in ElfAnt," log a journal entry, or start a focus session — hands-free, even while driving.
Day & Week Calendar
A real agenda view of your Google Calendar, with a tap-through to edit any event.
